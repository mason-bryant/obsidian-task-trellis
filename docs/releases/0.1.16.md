# Task Trellis 0.1.16

## Triage several tasks at once

Select cards individually, by column, or with **Select all** after applying filters. Change their column, due date, labels or priority together, with Undo for successful writes. Selected tasks stay selected even if the change removes them from the current filter.

- **Command-click** on Mac or **Ctrl-click** on Windows/Linux toggles a card; **Shift-click** selects a displayed range in the same column.
- Click empty board space or outside the board, press Escape, or choose **Done selecting** to leave selection mode.
- Actions and the green save checkmark stay above the scrolling content. Bulk editing applies to main tasks; child tasks remain unchanged.
- Tasks edited since selection are skipped rather than overwritten. Undo preserves notes that changed after the bulk action.

## Stay in context

- Open source notes and internal links in a pop-up editor over the board. Save and close, or expand into a regular note tab. Choose normal tabs in board settings if preferred.
- Failed saves retain the draft for retry, including during plugin disable. Escape and the native close-tab command save the popup without closing the board.
- Drag a subtask out into its own card, or use its Move menu on touch screens. Its own children stay with it.

## A cleaner board

- A compact **Subtasks** menu replaces the separate collapse/expand buttons.
- Quick filters that include labels show their label colors beside the filter name.
- New labels receive sequential default names. Stale label-editor saves are checked against the latest board settings.

## Validation and compatibility

484 plugin tests and 8 shortcut tests passed, along with lint, TypeScript and release packaging checks. Native macOS testing verified multi-note bulk writes, unchanged child tasks and exact Undo restoration; browser checks covered the 390px layout. The maintainer completed the phone smoke check and confirmed it looked good after the agent-operated Mirroring attempt proved unreliable. Android was not tested. See the [test report](../testing.md) for the detailed evidence and remaining coverage.

Requires Obsidian **1.13.7 or later**. No configuration migration is required.
