# Task Trellis 1.4.0

## Start a board from a template

**Create board** now asks what the board should start from, and shows a preview of each choice: the board it makes, drawn with sample tasks in its columns, rows and colors.

- **Kanban:** Backlog, Doing, Waiting and Done, with due dates as rows. This is the board Create board made before.
- **Weekly planner:** this week and next as rows, undated tasks in Someday, today's tasks tinted orange and high-priority ones striped red.
- **Project:** Backlog through Review to Done, labels for kinds of work, and cards labeled Blocked striped red.
- **Eisenhower matrix:** high priority means important, and due within two days means urgent. Each quadrant gets its own color and pattern.
- **Example board:** a dozen sample tasks in a note of their own, `Task Trellis examples/‹board name›.md`, to try things out. Delete the board and the note when you're done.

Each template makes an ordinary board, so you can change any of it later in **Settings**.

**When you first turn Task Trellis on** in a vault with no boards, the boards sidebar comes to the front listing the templates, and a notice points to it. Until now, turning it on showed nothing. See [Board templates](https://github.com/mason-bryant/obsidian-task-trellis/blob/1.4.0/docs/getting-started.md#board-templates).

## Patterns and opacity for color rules

- **Patterns:** a card color rule can use Stripes, Dots, Grid or Checks as well as a solid tint, so rules can be told apart without relying on color.
- **Opacity:** each rule can set its own strength, with a Preview card and a warning when text would be hard to read.
- **Color picker:** labels and color rules share Hue, Saturation and Lightness sliders, beside the palette and the HTML color code.

See [Color cards](https://github.com/mason-bryant/obsidian-task-trellis/blob/1.4.0/docs/filters.md#color-cards).

## What's new

A **What's new** button, just before **Settings** on a board's toolbar, lists recent features, with buttons and checkboxes to try them and a link to every release's notes. After an update that adds features, it opens by itself once, on the first board you open. Check **Don't show this again**, or turn off **Show what's new after updates** in **Settings → Task Trellis**, to stop that.

## Board settings, rearranged

The tabs are now **Appearance**, **Quick filters**, **Setup**, **New tasks** and **General**. Columns, Date rows and Filters moved into Setup, and Card color rules (formerly Colors) moved into Appearance. Settings reopen on the tab you last used.

## Smaller additions

- **Show completed subtasks** is in the toolbar's **Arrange** menu. It's the same setting as **Hide completed subtasks** in Appearance.
- **No due date column scrolls:** a board whose No due date row is a column can keep that column in view and scroll it on its own, with several columns showing. It's off until you choose **On its own**, the last option in Appearance, or tick its box in What's new.

## Fixes

**Safer edits and recovery**

- **Undo after a failed write** restores every note it can, names the ones it couldn't, and stays, so choosing it again tries just those. `task-trellis:undo` does the same.
- **Filters that can't be read,** such as a mapping under `include-tags`, used to show every task. Now the board, quick filter or color rule shows no tasks and warns. Saving something else keeps those rules, where it used to erase a quick filter's, and choosing new rules replaces them, which it didn't before.
- **A damaged rename recovery file** no longer stops the others being restored. Task Trellis names it at startup and in **Restore board rename**, and a new tag rename waits until it's dealt with.
- **Column widths:** saving an unrelated setting no longer erases the width of a column added in another window or by Sync.

**On the board**

- **Column commands:** on a board with a No due date column, **Add task to ‹board›: ‹column›** puts the task in the chosen column, by giving it the Due now row's last day.
- **Add task box:** typing no longer scrolls a desktop board. Opening another box keeps what you typed, ready for when you reopen the first, and Escape closes a box from anywhere inside it. Near the bottom of a wide board, the pinned box no longer covers a subtask's.
- **Long cells:** Done and No due date follow the same More and kept-card rules as other cells, and a long No due date column's outline and drop highlight cover the whole list.
- **Negative orders:** a hand-edited tag such as `#tt/<board>/<column>/-50` keeps the card in its column, first in its cell.
- **Selecting:** cards keep their strike-through and overdue bar. A click in the bulk bar, or a quick filter from the menu, no longer drops the selection, and a partial bulk edit names what it skipped.
- **Search** finds any completed subtask a card shows, and doesn't run on a word still being composed.
- **Boards and columns called Constructor or `__proto__`** keep their cards, moves, widths and collapsed rows.
- **A one-day partial week** is labeled with its day, such as **Sun, Nov 15**, instead of **Nov 15 – Nov 15**.

**Settings, touch and accessibility**

- **Pop-out windows:** dragging a column, quick filter or color rule in settings works there too.
- **Touch:** 44px tap areas around a card's checkbox and source link and around settings checkboxes.
- **Screen readers and keyboard:** repeated icon buttons name their task, general settings errors are said and take focus, and focus stays on the board after a redraw, the bulk editor and Undo.
- **Failures say so:** Sort by due date when it can't move things, a source link to a missing note, and a failed save of plugin settings. Task not saved can't be dismissed during a retry, and confirms where the task went.
- **Look:** the accent focus ring in dialogs, errors in the error color, and reduced motion in the sidebar and the touch drag copy.

## Compatibility

Requires Obsidian **1.13.7 or later**. No migration is needed: existing boards, tasks, tags and plugin settings are kept, and no note changes until you change something.

- **New board properties:** `undated-scroll`, and `opacity` and `pattern` in a color rule. Saving settings writes them only when you change them; a board made from a template writes its settings in full, as Create board always has. Earlier releases ignore them, so they show such a rule as a plain tint and scroll the No due date column with the board.
- **Filters that can't be read now hide tasks.** A board, quick filter or color rule whose properties hold something unusable, such as a mapping where a list belongs, shows no tasks and a warning. Earlier releases ignored it and showed tasks it was meant to hide. Fix the property in the board note, or choose the rules again in **Settings**.
- **The example board** writes its sample tasks to `Task Trellis examples/`, outside the boards folder, so boards that show tasks from every note show them too until you delete that note.
- **Plugin settings** gain which What's new you've seen, whether to show it after updates, and the last settings tab. Earlier releases ignore them.

## Validation

- **Automated:** 1001 application tests and 8 shortcut tests, lint, TypeScript checking, the harness build and packaging, locally and in CI.
- **Browser preview (the harness):** desktop and phone width (375 px), in dark and light themes. Checked: the empty boards sidebar, the New board dialog with each template's preview, choosing a template, and the partial-week label.
- **Native macOS, Obsidian 1.14.5,** in disposable vaults with synthetic tasks, with real clicks, keys and drags sent as input events:
  - **Across the board:** add task boxes and subtasks, completing and Undo, moving with the keyboard and by drag, collapsing rows, search, quick filters, the column picker, Arrange and Sort by due date (including a sort that couldn't write), selection and bulk edits, labels, the source popup, every settings tab, the new board dialog, commands, the sidebar, More, a No due date column on its own, and wide, narrow, light and zoomed layouts.
  - **Recovery and failures,** with a note made truly read-only: Undo and `task-trellis:undo` restored the other note, named the locked one and finished on a retry. A truncated and an incomplete recovery file were named while a good copy restored.
  - **Filters that can't be read,** written as real YAML: no cards and a warning, kept through unrelated saves, and replaced by new rules.
  - **Pop-out windows:** dragging columns, quick filters and color rules in a real pop-out failed on a build without the fix and worked with it.
  - **First run:** Task Trellis turned on in Obsidian's own Settings window, then a template chosen from the sidebar, and every template's preview clicked through.
  - **Updating from 1.3.0,** using the packaged 1.4.0 files over 1.3.0 built from its tag, in a vault where 1.3.0 had made a board, a color rule, a moved card and a collapsed row: What's new opened once with the three 1.4.0 features and not again, the board and task notes were unchanged, and the card, its color and the collapsed row were kept.
  - **A new install** of the packaged files: turned on with a click in Obsidian's Settings window, it brought the sidebar forward with its notice, made boards from the templates, and didn't open What's new.
  - **No side effects:** Obsidian's error log was clean.
- **Before tagging:** the same code was installed in the maintainer's own vault.
- **Not tested:**
  - Phones (iPhone and Android) and iPad, including the first run and the template previews on a phone.
  - Listening with VoiceOver.
  - Obsidian 1.13.7, the oldest version this release supports.
  - A physically held mouse (the drags above were synthesized input events), and the checkmark in macOS's native Arrange menu.
  - A real input method such as Japanese; search's handling of a word being composed was checked only with simulated composition events.
  - Windows and Linux, and real sync conflicts.
